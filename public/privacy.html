Volta Privacy

Privacy is built into Volta.

Your music requests go to the Navidrome server you choose. Local files and listening preferences stay in your browser unless you choose an optional service.

Open the player Get help

At a glance

The short version.

Direct by default

Library, artwork, lyrics, and playback requests go directly to the Navidrome server you enter.

Local by default

Listening history, recommendation signals, queue state, and preferences are kept in browser storage.

Optional by choice

External lyrics and music-link lookups happen only when you enable or use those features.

Data flows

What leaves your browser.

Volta is a client-side player. These are the destinations involved in normal use and the reason each one receives a request.

Navidrome

Volta sends authentication, library, artwork, lyrics, and playback requests to the server address you enter. Your server and its hosting provider may keep their own request logs.

When you connect

LRCLIB

If external lyrics are enabled and your server has no lyrics, Volta may send the current song title, artist, album, and duration to look up lyrics.

Optional

MusicBrainz

If you select Find credits in a song’s details, Volta sends that song’s title and artist to MusicBrainz. It never looks up credits automatically.

When you ask

Apple Music

When Apple Music is your share destination, Volta sends title, artist, album, and duration to Apple’s public iTunes Search endpoint to find a matching catalog link.

When you copy a link

Spotify

When Spotify is selected, Volta builds a Spotify search URL locally. Opening that URL sends the search to Spotify according to Spotify’s own policies.

When you open a link

Hosting layer

The web host and security layer may process standard web requests, security headers, and delivery logs. Volta does not intentionally send listening telemetry to a Volta server.

When you load a page

Your browser

What stays here.

Sign-in information

Your password is not saved. If you enable Remember this sign-in, Volta stores a signed Navidrome auth token in this browser profile. Disconnect or forget the account to remove it.

Listening and recommendations

Optional listening history, recommendation signals, search history, queue state, preferences, and interface settings are stored in your browser. Volta does not intentionally send this profile to a Volta server.

Local music

When you use This device, Volta reads selected files in your browser. Your local music is not uploaded by Volta. Browser and operating-system permissions still apply.

Cookies and analytics

The player does not require a Volta account or an analytics profile. Volta does not intentionally send listening telemetry to Volta.

Control

You decide what to share.

Optional services are visible in Settings, and local data can be cleared from the player.

Turn off external lyrics

Open Settings and disable external lyrics. Volta will use only lyrics provided by your Navidrome server.

Clear local data

Listening history and recommendation data can be disabled or cleared from Settings at any time.
